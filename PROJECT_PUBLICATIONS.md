# Selected scientific references for ADOPT project pages

Last curated: **30 October 2026**. Recent entries are selected, not necessarily the three newest papers worldwide. Foundational papers are judgement-based and need group review. Conference posters and abstracts appear in a separate resources section.

These references describe the projects or related technology. They do not establish that an individual paper has ADOPT authorship.
