Luca Cortese

Research contributor · University of Padova

Participates in space-surveillance research, including optical polarimetry for characterising geostationary satellites.

About

Luca participates in studies of space-object surveillance and characterisation. His research considers how optical polarimetry can help identify or describe the properties of geostationary satellites and other objects in orbit. His role illustrates the application of astronomical measurement techniques beyond traditional celestial science targets.

Projects

  • Space Debris

    Optical detection and characterisation of human-made objects in orbit.
