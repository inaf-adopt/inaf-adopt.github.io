About

Tania works on astronomical instrumentation and wavefront-sensing research associated with INAF Padova. Public presentations include SHARK-NIR science and the Ingot wavefront sensor, and she contributes to technical communication about instrument development.

Projects

  • INGOT

    Wavefront sensing tailored to elongated artificial guide stars.

  • SHARK-NIR

    Direct imaging and spectroscopy close to bright stars in the near-infrared.
