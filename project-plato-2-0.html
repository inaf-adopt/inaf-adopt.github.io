PLATO 2.0

The earlier PLATO 2.0 development concept preceding today’s ESA PLATO configuration.

PLATO 2.0 was an earlier programme name and design phase within the development of ESA’s exoplanet photometry mission. Historic presentations discussed optical camera architectures that were subsequently revised; it is not a separate contemporary space mission alongside PLATO.
