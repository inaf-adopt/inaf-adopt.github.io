VLT · Visible-light adaptive optics

MAVIS

Adaptive-optics-assisted visible-light imaging and spatially resolved spectroscopy.

MAVIS (MCAO-Assisted Visible Imager and Spectrograph) is being developed for the Very Large Telescope. It combines multi-conjugate adaptive optics with a visible imager and an integral-field spectrograph to overcome atmospheric image blur over a useful field of view.

Our contribution

Our work includes optical design, the adaptive-optics calibration strategy, system engineering and verification of the adaptive-optics module, in collaboration with the international MAVIS team.
