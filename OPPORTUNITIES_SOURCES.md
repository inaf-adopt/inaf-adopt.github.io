# Opportunities — editorial sources and review

Reviewed 31 October 2026. This website page describes general pathways and examples, not active ADOPT calls, awarded funding or available supervision.

- University of Padova official PhD calls: https://www.unipd.it/en/dottorato/bandi-graduatorie — admission is governed by formal calls and selection processes. Do not reproduce old deadlines.
- University of Padova PhD programmes: https://www.unipd.it/en/dottorato/elenco — university doctoral programme directory.
- University of Padova internships and traineeships: https://www.unipd.it/en/stage — curricular and extracurricular placements, host institutions and supervisory process.
- University of Padova science degrees: https://www.scienze.unipd.it/en/courses/degree-programs/astronomy/ — context for astronomy and physics studies.
- INAF opportunities/calls overview: https://www.media.inaf.it/inaftv/news/ — current institutional call notices; **not** a listing of ADOPT vacancies.

The example research subjects use the ADOPT research/project pages already on this site (INGOT, MATTO, MAVIS, SOXS), with no claim that a particular supervisor is currently accepting theses or interns.

Editorial procedure for an actual opening: before publishing, confirm title, topic, supervisor, host institution, eligibility, funding/salary, deadline, selection method and official application URL. Remove or archive calls after closing. For Master's thesis and internship enquiries, confirm degree-university rules and institutional approvals first.

No new email address, contact person or salary has been invented. The site directs readers to the existing team directory and official University/INAF sources.
