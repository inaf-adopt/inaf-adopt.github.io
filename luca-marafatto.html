Instrument integration

Luca Marafatto

Technologist · INAF Padova

Assembly, integration and verification manager for SHARK-NIR; also works on adaptive-optics and telescope instrumentation.

About

Luca works on integrating and commissioning astronomical instrumentation, including leading assembly, integration and verification activities for SHARK-NIR. He also contributes to laboratory and adaptive-optics projects, including MATTO and next-generation instrument programmes. His focus is the passage from optical designs and individual subsystems to working, tested instruments.

Projects

  • SHARK-NIR

    Direct imaging and spectroscopy close to bright stars in the near-infrared.

  • MORFEO

    An adaptive-optics relay enabling diffraction-limited imaging with MICADO.

  • MAVIS

    Adaptive-optics-assisted visible-light imaging and spatially resolved spectroscopy.
