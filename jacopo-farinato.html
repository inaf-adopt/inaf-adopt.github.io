Jacopo Farinato

Technologist · INAF Padova

Principal investigator of SHARK-NIR, with instrument engineering contributions to PLATO and MORFEO.

About

Jacopo is the principal investigator of SHARK-NIR, the near-infrared high-contrast camera at the Large Binocular Telescope. His work spans instrument design and development for high angular-resolution astronomy, together with collaborations involving optical systems for PLATO and MORFEO. These efforts support the observation of faint companions and circumstellar material close to bright stars.

Projects

  • SHARK-NIR

    Direct imaging and spectroscopy close to bright stars in the near-infrared.

  • PLATO

    Finding and characterising Earth-sized planets around bright stars.

  • MORFEO

    An adaptive-optics relay enabling diffraction-limited imaging with MICADO.
