Adaptive optics

Alessandro Ballone

Technologist · INAF Padova

Develops the MATTO multi-conjugate adaptive-optics test bench and its DAO4MATTO real-time control system.

About

Alessandro develops the MATTO multi-conjugate adaptive-optics laboratory at INAF Padova. His work includes the DAO4MATTO real-time control architecture, combining laboratory hardware, wavefront sensing and deformable-mirror control. The facility is designed to test several MCAO configurations and compare new control approaches before they are adopted by astronomical observatories.

Projects

  • MATTO

    A configurable laboratory bench for developing and comparing MCAO technologies.

  • MORFEO

    An adaptive-optics relay enabling diffraction-limited imaging with MICADO.
