EKARUS

A modular on-sky platform for testing new adaptive-optics technologies.

EKARUS is an experimental adaptive-optics research platform under development for the 1.82-m Copernico Telescope at Asiago. Building on experience from the PAPYRUS demonstrator, it is conceived as an accessible, flexible optical bench for testing new ideas on the sky.

Our contribution

Doctoral research in the group contributes to the development of this adaptive-optics experimentation platform at Asiago.
