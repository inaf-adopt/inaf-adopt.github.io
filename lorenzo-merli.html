Lorenzo Merli

Research contributor · University of Padova

Research contributor to multi-conjugate adaptive-optics test-bench development, including MATTO.

About

Lorenzo contributes to the MATTO multi-conjugate adaptive-optics laboratory programme at INAF Padova. Public workshop material lists him among the collaborators in the facility's design and early implementation. His listed interests are tied to optical test-bench integration and the development of systems used to experiment with multiple wavefront-sensing and correction configurations.
