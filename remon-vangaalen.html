Remon VanGaalen

Doctoral researcher · INAF Padova collaboration

Studies calibration and non-common-path aberration characterisation for NIRVANA-VIS, with wider adaptive-optics testing interests.

About

Remon undertakes doctoral research linked to advanced adaptive-optics instrumentation at INAF Padova. A public AO4ELT conference programme identifies his work on non-common-path aberration characterisation for NIRVANA-VIS. That topic connects calibration, optical modelling and test procedures to reliable performance of a complex telescope instrument.

Projects

  • NIRVANA-VIS

    A visible-light upgrade of LINC-NIRVANA, combining adaptive optics and speckle holography.

  • MAVIS

    Adaptive-optics-assisted visible-light imaging and spatially resolved spectroscopy.
