ELT · High-resolution infrared science

MICADO Science

Scientific opportunities enabled by the MICADO infrared imager and MORFEO.

MICADO is the high-resolution near-infrared camera and spectrograph planned for the ELT. Working with adaptive-optics correction from MORFEO, it is designed for exceptionally sharp imaging, accurate astrometry, coronagraphy and spectroscopy.

Our contribution

Research interests include reconstruction of the point-spread function: the way a point source appears in an image. This supports the interpretation of observations corrected by adaptive optics.
