Adaptive optics

Davide Greggio

Researcher · INAF Padova

Works on optical systems, wavefront sensing and multi-conjugate adaptive-optics instrumentation and laboratory test facilities.

About

Davide works on the optical design, modelling and laboratory verification of advanced astronomical systems. His collaborations include the Ingot laser-guide-star wavefront sensor, the MATTO MCAO test bench and high-resolution optical instrumentation. This work connects optical design to practical alignment, test measurements and performance assessment.

Projects

  • MAVIS

    Adaptive-optics-assisted visible-light imaging and spatially resolved spectroscopy.

  • MATTO

    A configurable laboratory bench for developing and comparing MCAO technologies.

  • SHARK-NIR

    Direct imaging and spectroscopy close to bright stars in the near-infrared.
