Instrumentation

Marco Dima

Technologist · INAF Padova

Research and engineering interests include wavefront sensing, high-contrast instrumentation and wide-field/space-surveillance concepts.

About

Marco contributes to astronomical instrumentation and adaptive-optics research at INAF Padova. Co-authored projects include laser-guide-star wavefront sensing, the MATTO test bench and SOXS optomechanical integration. He also works on optical space surveillance and instrument performance studies.

Projects

  • INGOT

    Wavefront sensing tailored to elongated artificial guide stars.

  • Mezzocielo

    A telescope concept designed to observe a very wide area of the sky.

  • Space Debris

    Optical detection and characterisation of human-made objects in orbit.
