# Publication selection

The Publications page defaults to selected papers with a current directory member in the first or second author position. Author order does not imply a particular contribution or affiliation at publication time. The wider project bibliography remains available through the All project literature option.

Verified on 31 October 2026. `ADOPT_PUBLICATION_AUTHORSHIP` in project-papers.js stores the first two authors, exact directory member IDs, positions and supporting source URLs. Sources are publisher-deposited Crossref metadata, institutional repositories, arXiv and conference records. Do not infer second authorship from an abbreviated et al. citation. Unverified entries remain in the wider bibliography; this is a curated selection, not a comprehensive author bibliography.

The 2023 Ingot conference abstract byline was corrected to Di Filippo et al. from the linked conference document.
