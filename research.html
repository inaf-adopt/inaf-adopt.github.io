Our research

We combine optical physics, laboratory experiments and systems engineering to develop and test astronomical instruments.

Adaptive optics

Adaptive optics measures changes in incoming light and corrects them in real time. We work on optical design, calibration and performance, including systems that use several guide stars and correcting mirrors to improve images across a wider field.

Wavefront sensing

A wavefront sensor measures distortions in light. Our work includes pyramid sensors, artificial guide stars, optical modelling and laboratory tests. These measurements help determine the corrections an adaptive-optics system should apply.

Astronomical instrumentation

We develop optical systems and help turn designs into working instruments. Our activities include alignment, calibration, systems engineering, assembly, integration and verification (AIV), and commissioning at telescopes.

What does adaptive optics change?

Atmospheric turbulence spreads a star’s light over a larger area. Adaptive optics measures the distortion and adjusts a mirror to help concentrate that light.
