Optical engineering

Demetrio Magrin

Senior technologist · INAF Padova

Designs and verifies astronomical optical systems, including the PLATO telescope optics, MORFEO and MezzoCielo.

About

Demetrio develops and validates precision optical systems for astronomical facilities. His work includes optical-engineering contributions to PLATO and to advanced ground-based instrumentation such as MORFEO, plus studies of unconventional wide-field telescope designs including MezzoCielo. Optical tolerancing, performance verification and collaboration across instrument subsystems are central themes in this work.

Projects

  • PLATO

    Finding and characterising Earth-sized planets around bright stars.

  • MORFEO

    An adaptive-optics relay enabling diffraction-limited imaging with MICADO.

  • Mezzocielo

    A telescope concept designed to observe a very wide area of the sky.
