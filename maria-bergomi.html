Astronomical instruments

Maria Bergomi

Technologist · INAF Padova

Project manager for SHARK-NIR; also contributes to adaptive-optics instrument programmes such as MAVIS and MORFEO.

About

Maria is involved in developing and delivering high-contrast astronomical instrumentation. Her work includes project management for SHARK-NIR at the Large Binocular Telescope, alongside collaborations on advanced adaptive-optics instruments such as MAVIS and MORFEO. These programmes bring precision optics, instrument verification and coordinated engineering to the search for faint objects near bright stars.

Projects

  • SHARK-NIR

    Direct imaging and spectroscopy close to bright stars in the near-infrared.

  • MAVIS

    Adaptive-optics-assisted visible-light imaging and spatially resolved spectroscopy.

  • MORFEO

    An adaptive-optics relay enabling diffraction-limited imaging with MICADO.
