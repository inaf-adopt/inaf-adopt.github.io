LBT · Near-infrared high-contrast imaging

SHARK-NIR

Direct imaging and spectroscopy close to bright stars in the near-infrared.

SHARK-NIR is a high-contrast near-infrared camera and spectrograph at the Large Binocular Telescope. It works with adaptive optics to separate faint structures and companions from the glare of a bright object, using coronagraphs to suppress starlight.

Our contribution

Group members contribute to project management, assembly, integration and verification, commissioning, and the preparation and analysis of observations within the SHARK-NIR collaboration.
