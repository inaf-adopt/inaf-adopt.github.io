Daniele Vassallo

Technologist · INAF Padova

Works on wavefront sensing, adaptive-optics systems and real-time control, including MATTO and high-contrast instrumentation.

About

Daniele works on wavefront sensing, adaptive optics and associated real-time instrument-control technologies. He contributes to the MATTO multi-conjugate adaptive-optics facility and its control architecture. His research also includes AO performance and experimental investigations for high-resolution astronomical observations.

Projects

  • MATTO

    A configurable laboratory bench for developing and comparing MCAO technologies.

  • SHARK-NIR

    Direct imaging and spectroscopy close to bright stars in the near-infrared.

  • MAVIS

    Adaptive-optics-assisted visible-light imaging and spatially resolved spectroscopy.
