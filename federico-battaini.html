Instrumentation

Federico Battaini

Doctoral researcher · INAF-associated

Research contributor to adaptive-optics test benches and astronomical instrumentation, including MATTO and SOXS.

About

Federico contributes to optical-instrument assembly, integration and verification, including SOXS at the New Technology Telescope. He has co-authored work on integrating and verifying the SOXS Common Path and participates in the wider MATTO laboratory collaboration. His research connects practical optomechanical testing with the commissioning of astronomical instruments.

Projects

  • MATTO

    A configurable laboratory bench for developing and comparing MCAO technologies.

  • SOXS

    Spectroscopic follow-up of supernovae and other rapidly changing objects.
