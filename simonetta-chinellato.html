Simonetta Chinellato

Senior technologist · INAF Padova

About

Simonetta works on technical and programme aspects of complex astronomical instrumentation at INAF Padova. Her published project associations include PLATO telescope development, SHARK-NIR and adaptive-optics initiatives. Her experience brings together instrument-development processes and coordination across participating teams.

Projects

  • PLATO

    Finding and characterising Earth-sized planets around bright stars.

  • SHARK-NIR

    Direct imaging and spectroscopy close to bright stars in the near-infrared.

  • MAVIS

    Adaptive-optics-assisted visible-light imaging and spatially resolved spectroscopy.
