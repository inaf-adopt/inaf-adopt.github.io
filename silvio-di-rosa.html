Silvio Di Rosa

Research contributor · INAF Padova

Studies the optomechanical design, optical materials and performance of the MezzoCielo telescope concept.

About

Silvio contributes to optical and optomechanical studies of the MezzoCielo wide-field telescope concept. The public literature discusses the performance of its unconventional spherical and refractive optical arrangement.

Projects
