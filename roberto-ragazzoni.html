Adaptive optics

Roberto Ragazzoni

Professor · University of Padova | President of INAF

Developed the pyramid wavefront sensor and works on adaptive optics and astronomical instrumentation.

About

Roberto’s research includes the pyramid wavefront sensor, adaptive optics and optical concepts for astronomical instruments, including MORFEO, MezzoCielo and PLATO.

Projects

  • MORFEO

    An adaptive-optics relay enabling diffraction-limited imaging with MICADO.

  • Mezzocielo

    A telescope concept designed to observe a very wide area of the sky.

  • PLATO

    Finding and characterising Earth-sized planets around bright stars.
