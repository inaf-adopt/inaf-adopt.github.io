MATTO

A configurable laboratory bench for developing and comparing MCAO technologies.

MATTO (Multi-conjugate Adaptive Techniques Test Optics) is a laboratory facility developed at INAF Padova under the STILES programme. It reproduces important optical and control aspects of multi-conjugate adaptive optics for large telescopes, allowing repeatable experiments before taking ideas on sky.

Our contribution

Our laboratory work includes the multi-conjugate adaptive-optics test bench, wavefront sensing, real-time control and experimental validation.
