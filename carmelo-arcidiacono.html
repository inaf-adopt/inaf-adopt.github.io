Adaptive optics

Carmelo Arcidiacono

Researcher · INAF Padova

Works on adaptive-optics development, simulations and instrument science, including the MORFEO programme.

About

Carmelo works on multi-conjugate adaptive optics and the prediction and reconstruction of the point-spread function in corrected astronomical images. His research connects numerical modelling with the MORFEO system for the Extremely Large Telescope, including methods that help astronomers interpret images after atmospheric correction. He is also associated with Padova's adaptive-optics laboratory efforts.

Projects

  • MORFEO

    An adaptive-optics relay enabling diffraction-limited imaging with MICADO.

  • MATTO

    A configurable laboratory bench for developing and comparing MCAO technologies.
