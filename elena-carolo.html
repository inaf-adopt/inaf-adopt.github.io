Elena Carolo

Technologist · INAF Padova

Contributes to integration and development of astronomical instruments, including SHARK-NIR and the MAVIS/MORFEO programmes.

About

Elena contributes to the engineering and development of optical instruments at INAF Padova. Her work includes SHARK-NIR, MAVIS, MORFEO and related laboratory integration activities. Such efforts require optical and mechanical integration, verification and coordination between scientific and technical teams.

Projects

  • SHARK-NIR

    Direct imaging and spectroscopy close to bright stars in the near-infrared.

  • MAVIS

    Adaptive-optics-assisted visible-light imaging and spatially resolved spectroscopy.

  • MORFEO

    An adaptive-optics relay enabling diffraction-limited imaging with MICADO.
