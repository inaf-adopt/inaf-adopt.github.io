Scientific sources

Project pages link to official collaborations, institutional descriptions and research papers. Specifications describe the cited instrument designs and may evolve. Bibliographies distinguish instrument research from observational science and do not imply authorship by every group member.

Illustrations and identity

The ADOPT graphic mark and schematic illustrations were created for this website. They are not official INAF logos. The adaptive-optics comparison is conceptual and is not a prediction of instrument performance.

Languages

English is the source text. The language menu provides automatic translation, including Italian; these translations have not been reviewed by the group. Scientific names and people’s names are protected from translation where possible.

Privacy and external services

Site searches run in your browser. The site does not add analytics. GTranslate loads third-party scripts to provide translation, and some portraits load from the MAVIS website. External services apply their own privacy policies.
