Mohamed Bournane

Doctoral researcher · University of Padova / INAF collaboration

Research focuses on optical corrector design for the MezzoCielo wide-field telescope demonstrator.

About

Mohamed works on optical design studies for innovative wide-field astronomical telescopes, including the MezzoCielo demonstration concept. His University of Padova academic record also includes an earlier study of the optical configuration for the proposed HAYDN space mission.
