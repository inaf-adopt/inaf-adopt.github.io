PLATO

Finding and characterising Earth-sized planets around bright stars.

PLATO (PLAnetary Transits and Oscillations of stars) is an ESA space mission designed to monitor the brightness of many stars with high precision. Periodic dimmings can reveal transiting planets, while stellar oscillations help determine the host stars’ ages and other properties.

Our contribution

Group members contribute to telescope optical design, systems engineering, technical assurance and integration within the wider PLATO collaboration.
