CHEOPS

Precise measurements of known exoplanets passing in front of bright stars.

CHEOPS (CHaracterising ExOPlanet Satellite) is an ESA space telescope that observes bright stars already known to host exoplanets. By measuring small decreases in starlight during transits, CHEOPS determines planet radii with high precision, helping researchers infer densities and compositions when masses are known.

This archive provides context for earlier involvement in space-camera optical development. Mission operations and scientific results are documented by ESA and the CHEOPS collaboration.
