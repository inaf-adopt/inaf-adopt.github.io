Previous involvement · Pyramid sensing beyond astronomy

WaterFall

An optical metrology prototype applying pyramid wavefront sensing to ophthalmology.

WaterFall is described in a University of Padova doctoral thesis as an application of pyramid wavefront-sensing principles to optical measurements in the human eye. The prototype was developed to measure the dioptric power of intraocular lenses.

Our contribution

This work applies pyramid wavefront-sensing methods from astronomical optics to measurements of the human eye.
