LINC-NIRVANA

A near-infrared interferometric camera combining the light from the LBT’s two mirrors.

LINC-NIRVANA was developed to combine near-infrared light from the two 8.4-m LBT mirrors and achieve the resolution possible from their much larger interferometric baseline. It pioneered layer-oriented, multi-conjugate adaptive optics using natural guide stars and pyramid wavefront sensing.

Our contribution

Group members contributed to optical alignment, wavefront sensing, integration and on-sky commissioning through the international LINC-NIRVANA collaboration.
