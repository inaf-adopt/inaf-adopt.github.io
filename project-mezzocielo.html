Mezzocielo

A telescope concept designed to observe a very wide area of the sky.

MezzoCielo is a proposed wide-field optical system built around a spherical, monocentric design. An envelope of meniscus lenses surrounds a transparent, low-index liquid; multiple corrected cameras and detectors sample different viewing directions.

Our contribution

Group members study the optical configuration, corrector design, optomechanics and performance of the wide-field telescope demonstrator.
