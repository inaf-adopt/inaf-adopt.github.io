Oleksandra Rebrysh

Research contributor · INAF Padova

Optical designer working on the final design and verification of the MAVIS adaptive-optics module.

About

Oleksandra works on optical design and performance verification for the MAVIS adaptive-optics module at the Very Large Telescope. Her research examines the post-focal relay and wavefront-sensor optical systems, including their design requirements and measured or simulated image quality. Public workshop materials present the final optical design and the supporting optical analyses.
