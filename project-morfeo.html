ELT · Multi-conjugate adaptive-optics relay

MORFEO

An adaptive-optics relay enabling diffraction-limited imaging with MICADO.

MORFEO is a first-generation multi-conjugate adaptive-optics system for ESO’s Extremely Large Telescope. Instead of recording science images itself, it corrects distorted incoming light and passes it to scientific instruments, initially MICADO.

Our contribution

Our activities include optical design, adaptive-optics modelling and preparation for assembly, integration, verification and testing at the telescope Nasmyth platform, including coordination with the laser-guide-star wavefront-sensor team.
