NIRVANA-VIS

A visible-light upgrade of LINC-NIRVANA, combining adaptive optics and speckle holography.

NIRVANA-VIS develops a visible-light channel for LINC-NIRVANA at the Large Binocular Telescope. It combines ground-layer adaptive optics with speckle holography, a method for reconstructing images from short exposures.

Our contribution

Our contributions include systems engineering, calibration and optical-aberration characterisation, laboratory integration and commissioning planning, including doctoral research.
