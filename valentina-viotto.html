Adaptive-optics engineering

Valentina Viotto

Technologist · INAF Padova

Contributes to adaptive-optics instrument development, including MAVIS, and systems engineering and product development for PLATO.

About

Valentina works on astronomical instrumentation and adaptive-optics development at INAF Padova. Her collaborations include the design and verification of MAVIS systems, the MATTO multi-conjugate adaptive-optics laboratory and optical-engineering contributions to the PLATO mission. Her work supports the translation of technical requirements into tested optical systems.

Projects

  • MAVIS

    Adaptive-optics-assisted visible-light imaging and spatially resolved spectroscopy.

  • PLATO

    Finding and characterising Earth-sized planets around bright stars.

  • INGOT

    Wavefront sensing tailored to elongated artificial guide stars.
