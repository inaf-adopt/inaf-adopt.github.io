Wavefront sensing

Simone Di Filippo

Research contributor · INAF Padova

Works on wavefront sensors, adaptive-optics control and laboratory validation, including the Ingot WFS and MATTO.

About

Simone studies wavefront-sensing techniques for large telescopes, particularly the Ingot sensor for elongated sodium laser guide stars. His work includes optical design, automated prism alignment, laboratory tests and sensitivity to introduced low-order aberrations. These results support the evaluation of alternatives to conventional wavefront sensing on extremely large telescopes.

Projects

  • INGOT

    Wavefront sensing tailored to elongated artificial guide stars.

  • MATTO

    A configurable laboratory bench for developing and comparing MCAO technologies.

  • SHARK-NIR

    Direct imaging and spectroscopy close to bright stars in the near-infrared.

  • SOXS

    Spectroscopic follow-up of supernovae and other rapidly changing objects.
