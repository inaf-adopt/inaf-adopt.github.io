NTT · Optical and near-infrared spectroscopy

SOXS

Spectroscopic follow-up of supernovae and other rapidly changing objects.

SOXS (Son Of X-Shooter) is a spectrograph on the New Technology Telescope at La Silla, built to rapidly classify and study astronomical transients. It obtains ultraviolet/visible and near-infrared spectra over a broad wavelength range.

Our contribution

Our contributions include leadership of assembly, integration and verification (AIV), deputy systems engineering, instrument installation and commissioning at La Silla, and preparation for operations within the SOXS consortium.
