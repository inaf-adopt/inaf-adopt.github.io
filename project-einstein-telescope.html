Einstein Telescope

A next-generation underground observatory for signals from cosmic mergers.

The Einstein Telescope is a proposed European, third-generation gravitational-wave observatory. Its extremely sensitive laser interferometers are designed to detect the tiny changes in distance caused by passing gravitational waves, extending observations much farther into the Universe.

Research context

Optical measurement and precision instrumentation connect this research area with the group’s interests. The official project website describes the observatory and its collaboration.
