AO science & surveillance

Matteo Simioni

Research fellow · INAF Padova

Studies adaptive-optics point-spread-function reconstruction and optical surveillance of space objects.

About

Matteo is a research fellow associated with INAF Padova. His research includes adaptive-optics point-spread-function reconstruction and methods for interpreting images corrected by complex AO systems. He also works on optical observation and analysis of artificial objects in Earth orbit.

Projects

  • MICADO Science

    Scientific opportunities enabled by the MICADO infrared imager and MORFEO.

  • Space Debris

    Optical detection and characterisation of human-made objects in orbit.
